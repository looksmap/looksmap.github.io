Privacy Policy

looksmap · Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Contact: support@getlooksmap.com

looksmap is a price-comparison and information app for medical aesthetics. This policy says what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

What we collect

Account. The email address or phone number you sign in with (a phone number is used only to text you sign-in codes), a display name, your date of birth (used once to verify you are 18 or older), and the city you browse. If you continue with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and name that service shares with us, and nothing else from your account there. Your date of birth is stored separately from your public profile and is never shown to anyone.

Content you post. Treatment Receipts — the treatment, clinic, price paid, rating, review text, and any photos you attach. Published receipts are public and appear with your display name and profile photo. You can delete any receipt, or your whole account, in the app at any time.

Likes and follows. Which receipts you like and whom you follow are stored with your account so they carry over to a new phone. Other people see how many likes a receipt has, never who gave them, and no one sees whom you follow.

Usage data. Which screens and features are used, taps on listings, and how long pages are viewed — tied to a random install identifier and, when you are signed in, your account ID. We use this to understand which features are useful. It never includes the text of your reviews or photos.

Searches. What you search for is stored with your account (or anonymously if signed out) so we can see what people look for and fill gaps in the catalog. Search text is kept in our own database only and is never sent to third-party analytics.

Photos. Receipt photos you attach are stored so they can be shown with your receipt, and your profile picture so it can be shown with your name. Before a receipt is published or a profile picture is shown, the photo is screened automatically for nudity and other content that cannot be posted. A flagged receipt photo is held for a person on our team to review; a flagged profile picture is deleted and not used. Skin Check and "What's your aesthetic?" photos are analysed to produce your result and then discarded — they are never stored.

Concierge chats. Messages you send the concierge are answered by an AI. The conversation is kept on your phone. If you choose "Send to the team", the whole conversation and your email address are sent to us and kept so we can reply and follow up.

Location. If you allow location access, your position is used on your device to sort listings by distance. Your precise location is not uploaded or stored. The city you choose to browse is attached to usage data.

Who processes it

Our backend (database, authentication, and photo storage) runs on Supabase. Product analytics may be processed by PostHog. The AI features — the concierge, Skin Check, "What's your aesthetic?", and photo screening of receipt and profile pictures, and translating receipt reviews and comments between English and Chinese — are processed by Anthropic (Claude), which receives the photo, conversation or post needed for that one request. Emails from us, including replies to concierge requests, are sent through Resend. Sign-in codes are texted through Twilio. All of them act as processors for us. We do not sell personal data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Clinics and prices

Clinic listings and prices come from clinics' own published materials. They are not personal data about you, and nothing about your browsing is shared with clinics.

Deleting your data

Delete any receipt from its page, or delete your entire account from Profile → Delete account. Account deletion removes your profile, receipts, photos, comments, likes, follows, saves, and points immediately. You can also email support@getlooksmap.com for access or deletion requests.

Age

looksmap is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note it here with a new date.
