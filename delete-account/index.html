Delete your account

looksmap · Contact: support@getlooksmap.com

In the app

Open the Profile tab and choose Delete account. Deletion is immediate and removes your profile, Treatment Receipts, photos, comments, likes, follows, saves, and Aura Points. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email support@getlooksmap.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Everything tied to your account is deleted: account details, posts and photos, comments, likes, follows, saves, points, and search history linked to you. Nothing about your account is kept after deletion, except records we are legally required to retain, if any. Anonymous usage statistics that never identified you are unaffected.

You can also delete a single Treatment Receipt from its own page — hold the ••• menu — without deleting your account.
