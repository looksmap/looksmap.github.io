Support

Email support@getlooksmap.com. A person reads every message and replies by email.

A price is wrong or out of date

Every price shows the clinic's own source and the date it was captured. Clinics change prices without telling us, so a price can be out of date. Email the clinic, the treatment and what it charges now, and we correct the listing.

Something on a receipt or comment shouldn't be there

On a Treatment Receipt, tap •••. On a comment, press and hold it. Pick a reason and send the report; it disappears from your feed straight away. Tick Also block to stop seeing that person's receipts and comments.

Signing in

looksmap accounts are for people 18 and over. You can sign in with email or Google from the Profile tab, and with Apple on iPhone. If a sign-in email doesn't arrive, check spam, then email us from that address.

Deleting your account

Open Profile and choose Delete account, or see Delete your account.

For clinics

To correct or add your published prices, email us from your clinic's address with a link to where the prices are published.
