Refund policy

Serve sells digital items: Thread, Today’s edit and the Monthly card. Payments are handled by Stripe and appear on your statement as SERVE. The seller is looksmap, Inc.

Delivery is immediate

Items are added to your game as soon as you come back from checkout. By paying, you ask for them to be delivered straight away and agree that, once they have been added, you can no longer cancel the purchase just because you changed your mind.

When we refund in full

  • You were charged but the items never arrived.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • The purchase was made by mistake or without your permission, and you tell us within 14 days.

Other requests are looked at case by case. Nothing here takes away rights you have under the consumer law where you live.

How to ask

Email support@getlooksmap.com with the receipt number from the email Stripe sent you and what went wrong. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Your progress lives in this browser. Thread and items are saved on this device. Clearing your browser data, using private browsing or switching devices can remove them, and we can’t restore them from our side. If that happens straight after a purchase, email us and we’ll help.
