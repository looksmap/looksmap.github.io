Serve · Privacy

Privacy policy

Serve is a styling game made by looksmap, Inc. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Serve at looksmap.github.io/serve or install it to your home screen. Questions go to support@getlooksmap.com.

The short version. Serve has no accounts. Your progress stays in your browser. We count anonymous statistics, such as how many people play and which levels they finish, without cookies and without anything that identifies you. If you buy something, Stripe handles the payment, and we receive a record of that purchase. We don’t show ads, sell your information, or track you on other sites.

What stays on your device

Serve saves these things in your browser’s storage on the device you play on. They are not sent to us or to GoatCounter.

  • Your progress: level, Thread, wardrobe, saved looks, the outfits you’ve entered and their scores.
  • Your stylist: the look you pick and any name you type in.
  • A random player ID, such as p_k3x9…. It isn’t linked to your name, email or device.
  • A list of your purchases (the Stripe receipt number, item and price), so items aren’t added twice.
  • A log of your last 200 actions in the game, such as opening the shop or finishing a brief. It’s used for the You tab and stays on your device.
  • How you first found Serve: the website or link that sent you, if your browser shares it.

Clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the home-screen app, deletes all of this. We can’t see or restore it.

Anonymous statistics

To see how many people play and which parts of the game work, Serve counts visits and game events with GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service. Each time you open the game, GoatCounter receives:

  • the page, the website or link that sent you, and any campaign tag in that link;
  • your browser, operating system, screen size and language;
  • your country or region, worked out from your IP address. GoatCounter doesn’t store the IP address itself.

While you play, it also receives game events without any details about you: for example that a level was passed or failed, that the shop or checkout was opened, which item was bought, that a look was shared, or that the game was installed to a home screen.

These statistics don’t use cookies and don’t include your name, the name you type for your stylist, your player ID, your email or your payment details. GoatCounter tells repeat visits apart for a short time using a hash it can’t reverse and doesn’t keep. We see only totals. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

When you buy something

Purchases are made through Stripe, our payment processor. When you check out, you enter your payment details on Stripe’s own page, not in Serve. Stripe collects your card or wallet details, email address and billing country, and uses them under the Stripe privacy policy.

We never see your full card number. From Stripe we receive a record of the purchase: the item, the amount, the date, your email address, your billing country, the last four digits and brand of the card, and your random player ID, so a purchase can be matched to the right game. We use this to deliver items, answer support requests, handle refunds and prevent fraud. We keep these records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Other services that see your device

  • GitHub Pages hosts the game. Like any website host, it receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the game’s fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load. See the Google privacy policy.
  • GoatCounter receives the anonymous statistics described above.
  • Stripe, as above, only when you open a checkout page.

Serve itself doesn’t set cookies. Stripe’s checkout pages use their own cookies for security and fraud prevention.

Sharing your looks

When you share a look, the picture is made on your device and passed to your device’s share menu or clipboard. It goes only where you choose to send it. We don’t receive a copy.

Children

Serve is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. While playing, the game only sends the anonymous statistics described above, which can’t identify anyone. Buying something sends information to Stripe and to us, so purchases must be made by an adult cardholder or with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child under 13 has bought something in Serve, email us. We’ll refund it and delete what we hold about the purchase, except what the law requires us to keep.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. The only personal information we hold is the purchase records described above. To make a request, email support@getlooksmap.com from the address you used at checkout, or include your Stripe receipt number. We’ll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change what Serve collects, for example by adding analytics that send information to us, we’ll update this page before the change goes live and change the date below.
