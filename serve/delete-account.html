Delete your account

This page is for Serve, the styling game made by looksmap, Inc. It explains how to delete your Serve account and the copy of your progress that is saved with it.

In the game

  1. Open Serve. This can be the iPhone app, the Android app, or the website at looksmap.github.io/serve.
  2. If you are not signed in, open the You tab and sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before.
  3. On the You tab, choose Delete account, then Delete my account.

You don’t need the app to do this. Signing in on the website with the same Apple or Google account reaches the same account.

What is deleted

  • Your account record, which is a one-way code made from your Apple or Google account ID.
  • The copy of your saved game kept with it: your progress, your stylist, your random player ID and your list of purchases.

Both are deleted from our save service straight away and can’t be brought back.

What is not deleted

  • The progress stored on your own device. Use Reset all progress on the You tab, or remove the app, to delete that too.
  • Records of purchases, which Stripe, Apple or Google keep under their own policies, and which we keep where tax and accounting law requires.

If you can’t sign in

Email support@getlooksmap.com. We don’t keep your name or email address with your account, so we will ask you to sign in once on the website so the right account can be found and deleted.
