How your outfit is scored. Serve looks at nine things: how well you matched the brief, your colours, the shape of the outfit, the dress code, the weather, your fabrics, your standout piece, how finished the outfit is, and its style. Your outfit is compared with another stylist’s and you get a grade from S to D. There is nothing to download, because the game runs in your browser.
